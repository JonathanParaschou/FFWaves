# Audio, supply box and player scale

The public Vercel music URLs returned actual Ogg files identical to the local recordings. Decoding the six files with libsndfile confirmed non-silent PCM. The problem was not just absent files: Tech Center measured -26.4 dB RMS before a 0.65 music bus and 0.22 master, about -43.3 dB after mixing. Playback now balances original recordings by RMS with a peak cap, raises the master to 0.65 and applies a final compressor. Source recordings remain unchanged. Sound recovery retries on subsequent pointer/key gestures while respecting explicit mute. Settings show music/loading/failure status and provide Test Sound. Effects decoding runs four requests at a time instead of launching 297 together.

Nano recordings were also addressed by gameplay slugs (`summon-buttercup-`) while extracted files use original numeric IDs (`summon-1-` for Buttercup). The playback path now uses the catalog's `originalId`. The regression check resolves the real catalog and verifies recorded voice prefixes exist for supported Nanos.

The box body intersected the reference's angled brush 216. Its fixture moves 1.4 m inward and turns along the wall, with shared rotated collision bounds and a relocated interaction approach. This equipment adaptation is recorded in the source packet. It uses the original textured FusionFall crate, a blue idle beacon, question-mark hologram/minimap icon, arrival animation, opening lid, cycling weapons and a raised rarity-colored offer. The existing authoritative 950-taro / 3.1-second roll / 12-second pickup mechanics remain shared by solo and multiplayer. This bunker has a fixed box location; random relocation is not implemented.

The source has a 3.2 m ground-floor ceiling and 3.6 m floor rise. The player remains a 1.8 m hull with a 1.68 m eye. The old 5.25 m/s walk, 8.1 m/s sprint and 10.8 m/s slide made a ten-metre room pass in about 1.9/1.2 seconds. Walking now takes about 2.55 seconds and sprinting about 1.72 seconds, including acceleration. Normal jump height falls from about 1.14 m to 0.66 m. FOV changes from 75 vertical (about 107 horizontal at 16:9) to a default 95 horizontal (about 63 vertical); the separate weapon camera stays independently framed. Exact values are authored for this game.

COD references inform the behavior: the official [BO6 Zombies deep dive](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2024/08/call-of-duty-black-ops-6-zombies-deep-dive-terminus-map-intel) describes chaining sprint/slide movement and choosing maneuvering areas; the [BO7 deep dive](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2025/09/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-zombies-deep-dive) describes a physical Mystery Box and a 950-point random weapon spin. These sources do not publish the physics measurements used here.

Visual projectile travel was compressed into a few frames at room distances. The updated effects keep textured rounds visible for at least 100 ms (energy rounds 140 ms), add depth-tested trails, and leave damage authoritative and immediate. Visual muzzle projection accounts for the separate weapon/world FOVs; third-person shots originate from the hero's weapon. Effects release all temporary geometry/materials.

Validation includes actual recording decoding/RMS, shots at 144/60/30/20 Hz, movement distance/jump checks at 144/60/30 Hz, mute/resume and settings migration, real catalog voice lookup, physical box/source-volume samples, rotated visual/collision bounds, both source staircases, supply access, and existing online-room checks. Offline actual-scene views were regenerated and inspected. Computer Use reported neither a native pipe nor a browser host, so live WebGL, final listening levels, frame rate and human control feel remain unverified. Automated checks do not establish those qualities.

Final checkout build: 137 tests, 136 passed, one optional real-Redis test skipped. The box review also exposed the original crate roof underneath the animated lid; that roof is now separated into the moving lid while preserving its original UVs/materials and the cached asset. Reproduce its three states with `python tools/review-supply-box.py`.
